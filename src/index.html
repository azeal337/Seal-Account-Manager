    Graphics profiles

    Choose more FPS, a balance, or higher image quality. Compare one change at a time in the same game.

    Create a preset, save it, and select it on an account before launching. Choose Off on that account to remove its preset flags.

    Higher resolution and GPU settings

    NVIDIA DSR/DLDSR and AMD VSR render at a higher resolution for image quality, usually at an FPS cost. Configure these in your GPU software if supported; Seal does not change driver or display settings. Sharpening changes apparent crispness and does not create new texture detail.

    For a fair comparison, keep the game, camera, graphics level, FPS cap, and number of running accounts the same. Compare FPS and stutters, not just a single peak reading. Renderer changes require a new Roblox launch.
