# Getting started

1. Download and open the portable Windows executable from the project's [Releases](https://github.com/azeal337/Seal-Account-Manager/releases).
2. Click **Add account**, then complete Roblox sign-in and any verification.
3. Choose Play on an account card, or select several accounts and click **Launch selected**. Leave the destination empty for Roblox Home, or enter a game link, place ID, username, or server link.
4. Set FPS, graphics, volume, and performance on each account. **Apply** saves the settings and relaunches that account. **Bulk settings** can change selected options across a folder or multiple accounts.

## Background and external Play

Open **Launcher → Roblox settings** to choose whether SAM keeps running when its window closes and whether it starts hidden at Windows sign-in. Open SAM again from the tray. **Quit SAM** ends tracking.

When SAM's launcher handling is enabled, supported Roblox Home and website Play requests can route through it in the background. **Roblox Play settings** selects the settings profile to apply. Roblox retains the login used by the original request.

## Tracking and badges

Open **Time** for Roblox time by account or the **Games** library. Roblox Home and loading count toward Roblox time. A window SAM cannot match is listed as **Unlinked Roblox** until it can be linked. SAM records sessions it observes while running.

Games with an earned badge can appear with **0s** of recorded playtime. Badge ownership is lifetime data. First-play dates use the earliest recorded session or known badge award, and badge-based dates appear as estimates. Use **Sync badges** to refresh ownership and retry award-date lookups.

## Appearance and repair

Choose **Customize → Appearance** to preview a preset, then **Save changes**. Lord of the Mysteries includes Grey Fog and tarot panels. Select **Full effects** to animate the scene.

In **Launcher → Roblox settings**, use **Check Roblox** to review the install. **Repair join connection** and **Reset join cache** address different local join problems and have separate result messages.
